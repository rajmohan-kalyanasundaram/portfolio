About

Profile

Associate Professor at PERI Institute of Technology, Chennai, with over 11 years of teaching experience in electronics and communication engineering. My research focuses on wavelet-based image segmentation, medical and satellite image processing, signal processing, and VLSI design. I use MATLAB to develop and evaluate algorithms for filtering, feature extraction, noise reduction, edge detection, and pattern recognition.

Research

Research

My doctoral research investigated wavelet-based image segmentation techniques. My broader research work applies wavelet transforms, watershed methods, and related image-processing algorithms to medical and satellite imagery, with interests in signal-processing applications and efficient VLSI implementations.
